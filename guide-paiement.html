How to Pay with Crypto (Beginner Friendly)

New to crypto? Don't worry! Paying with USDT is just like sending money from a digital bank. Follow these simple steps to get your software in minutes.

1. NETWORK: TRON (TRC-20) ONLY

Do NOT send funds via Ethereum (ERC-20), BNB Chain (BEP-20), or any other network. Sending assets through an unsupported network will result in permanent loss of your funds.

2. PLEASE PAY THE EXACT AMOUNT OF 99 USDT

Any incomplete payment could lead to delays or complications in processing your request. However, if you mistakenly transfer more than the required amount, the excess will be refunded to you within 3 to 5 hours.

1

Get a Crypto Wallet

To buy crypto, you need an app. We recommend Binance because it is very fast and easy for beginners. (Note: This is just an example. You can also use other similar apps like Kraken, Trust Wallet, or MoonPay if you prefer).

Download the app on your phone, create an account, and verify your identity.

2

Buy USDT with your Bank Card

Once your account is ready, click on "Buy Crypto".

Choose USDT (Tether). You can use your Credit Card, Debit Card, or Bank Transfer to deposit money. Tip: Buy a little more than 99 USDT (like 101 USDT) to cover the small transfer fee charged by the app.

3

Send 99 USDT to our Address

Go to your wallet, select your USDT, and click Withdraw or Send.

Paste this exact address into the "Recipient Address" box:

4

Get your Software Immediately

After your transfer is successful, your app will give you a receipt called a TxID (Transaction ID). Please copy this TxID.

Click the button below to go to our final payment confirmation page. Enter your Email address and paste the TxID.
